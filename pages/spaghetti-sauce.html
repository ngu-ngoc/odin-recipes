Spaghetti Sauce

Home

Description

  • Time: About 45 minutes
  • Rating: 10
  • Notes: Read community notes

This homemade pasta sauce recipe is SO SIMPLE, yet so very good. To make it, I use ground beef, olive oil, onion, butter, crushed tomatoes, Italian seasoning blend, and salt and pepper. That’s it. It’s kind of like a shortcut Bolognese but ready in a fraction of the time, so you can get dinner on the table fast during the week.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb. ground beef
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 28 oz. can crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning blend
  • black pepper
  • salt

Steps

  1. Saute the ground beef and olive oil in a pot until the beef is cooked through. Drain any excess fat.
  2. Dice the onion.
  3. Add diced onion and butter to beef until onion is soft and translucent.
  4. Add crushed tomatoes, Italian seasoning blend, and black pepper; stir everything to combine.
  5. Place a lid on the pot and allow it to simmer for 30 minutes.
  6. Salt to taste.
