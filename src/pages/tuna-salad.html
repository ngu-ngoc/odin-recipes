Tuna Salad

Home

Description

  • Time: About 10 minutes
  • Rating: 7
  • Notes: Read community notes

This Classic Tuna Salad is creamy, fresh, and packed with texture thanks to crunchy walnuts, bright lemon, and simple ingredients. It's perfect for sandwiches, wraps, salads, or easy lunches.

Ingredients

  • 2 12 oz cans chunk light tuna in water
  • 1 cup diced celery
  • 1/4 cup walnuts
  • 2 green onions
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Steps

  1. Drain the canned tuna.
  2. Dice the celery, chop the walnuts, and slice the green onions.
  3. Add all ingredients to a large bowl and stir until well-combined.
